# Russian Case System — 4-Week Intensive Review Plan

**Coursebook:** В.С. Ермаченкова, «Повторяем падежи и предлоги» (Златоуст, 2014), 172 p.
**Your level:** B2+–C1 (this is a *drill-and-error-correct* pass, not new grammar)
**Cadence:** 45–60 min/day, ~4 weeks (28 days), self-study + testing with me
**Goal:** make the full case/preposition system automatic and error-free under advanced-vocabulary load.

---

## 1. How this plan works

The book is an A2 corrective workbook. For you it is a **scaffold of drills, not a source of new grammar**. You already know the forms; the job is fluency and zero-error output.

Two layers run in parallel every day:

1. **Self-drill (the book).** You work the book's exercises in writing to re-automate the forms. Check yourself against the answer keys (pp. 166–172).
2. **Testing (with me).** I test you with **my own sentences** — same grammatical skeleton as the day's sub-topic, but B2+–C1 vocabulary (abstract nouns, higher-register verbs, analytical/technical register). You respond in writing; I correct you.

Page numbers below are the book's **printed** page numbers (shown in the footer). PDF page = printed page + 1.

## 3. Daily routine (45–60 min)

| Time | Block | What |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 min | **Recall drill** | From memory, write the day's paradigm (noun + adjective + pronoun in the target case, sg & pl). Check against Appendices 2–3. |
| 10–30 min | **Book drill** | Work the day's exercises in writing, in order, stopping at the sub-topic boundary. |
| 30–40 min | **Elevated production** | Rewrite 5–8 of the day's sentences with B2+–C1 vocabulary, or write original sentences using the day's case. |
| 40–50 min | **Self-check** | Correct against keys. Log **every** error (not just typos — rule violations). |
| 50–60 min | **Spaced review** | Re-do yesterday's error-log items + 5 min flash recall of the previous case. |

## 6. Elevated-vocabulary method (B2+–C1)

The book's A2 sentences lean on concrete everyday nouns (*книга, продукты, музыка*). Keep the **grammatical slot**, upgrade the **lexical load**.

Before → after examples (data/analytical register, since that's your home turf):

| Case | Book-style (A2) | Upgrade (B2+–C1) |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Accusative | Я читаю книгу. | Я анализирую динамику спроса и обосновываю целесообразность инвестиций. |
| Genitive | У меня нет времени. | У руководства не оказалось достаточных оснований для пересмотра стратегии. |
| Prepositional | Мы говорим о погоде. | Мы рассуждали о целесообразности внедрения и о рисках, связанных с масштабированием. |
| Dative | Я даю книгу другу. | Мы предоставили доступ к данным независимому аудитору. |
| Instrumental | Я пишу ручкой. | Исследование подтвердило гипотезу с высокой степенью достоверности. |

Vocabulary pools to draw from (pick a theme each day): **business/analytics** (выручка, целесообразность, допущение, погрешность, выборка, масштабирование, удержание), **abstract nouns** (достоверность, сопоставимость, устойчивость, предпосылка, расхождение), **higher-register verbs** (обосновать, сформулировать, сопоставить, выявить, опровергнуть, пренебречь).

## 7. The 28-day plan

### Week 1 — Accusative (Винительный), pp. 4–33

- **Day 1 — Setup + inanimate nouns, sg.** Read Appendices 2–3 once (orientation). Drill accusative of inanimate masculine/neuter/feminine singular (Урок 1, point 1). Vocab theme: business/analytics objects. Test with me: 5 sentences, inanimate sg.
- **Day 2 — Inanimate nouns, pl + days of the week.** Point 1 continued; drill plural inanimates and time expressions (в понедельник, в среду). Test: inanimate pl.
- **Day 3 — Animate nouns, sg & pl.** Point 2. The key rule: animate accusative = genitive form (вижу студента, студентов). Test: animate sg + pl.
- **Day 4 — Pronouns, adjectives, numeral один.** Point 3: possessives (мой/твой…), demonstratives (этот/тот), определительные (весь/каждый), adjectives, and один in accusative. Test: mixed modifiers.
- **Day 5 — который in accusative.** Point 4: complex sentences (которого/которую/которые). Test: relative clauses.
- **Day 6 — Full accusative mixed drill.** Redo every error-log item from the week; produce one paragraph (150+ words) deliberately loading accusative. Self-check.
- **Day 7 — Review + diagnostic.** 20-minute diagnostic I give you (all accusative sub-points, B2+ vocab). Audit the week's error log; migrate recurring rules to a "rules card."

### Week 2 — Genitive (Родительный), pp. 34–73 *(the heaviest lesson)*

- **Day 8 — Genitive nouns, sg.** Point 1. Drill the core uses: possession, negation (нет), после/для/у/из/от + gen. Test: sg nouns across these prepositions.
- **Day 9 — Genitive nouns, pl.** Point 1 continued — the hardest surface in Russian (много/мало/сколько + gen pl; деньги, люди, годы). Test: quantity + gen pl.
- **Day 10 — Numbers & quantity.** Point 1 continued: 2–4 + gen sg vs 5+ + gen pl; сколько/несколько/много. Test: number + noun agreement.
- **Day 11 — Genitive adjectives & pronouns.** Point 2: adjectival endings and это/тот/мой in genitive, sg & pl. Test: modifier agreement.
- **Day 12 — который in genitive.** Point 3: relative clauses (которого/которой/которых). Test: relative clauses.
- **Day 13 — Full genitive mixed drill.** Redo error-log items; paragraph (150+ words) loading genitive. Self-check.
- **Day 14 — Review + diagnostic.** Accusative vs genitive contrast test from me; audit error log; update rules card.

### Week 3 — Prepositional (Предложный, pp. 74–103) + Dative (Дательный, pp. 104–125)

- **Day 15 — Prepositional of place.** Урок 3, point 1: в/на + prepositional, location (где?). Drill жить в/на. Test: place expressions.
- **Day 16 — Prepositional of object.** Point 2: о/об + prepositional, speech/thought verbs (говорить/думать/спорить о…). Test: object expressions.
- **Day 17 — Prepositional pronouns/adjectives + который.** Points 3–5. Test: combined.
- **Day 18 — Dative nouns, sg & pl.** Урок 4, point 1: recipient (кому?), к + dative, нужен/нравиться constructions. Test: recipient constructions.
- **Day 19 — Dative pronouns & adjectives.** Points 2–3: personal pronouns (мне/тебе…), modifiers. Test: pronoun + modifier agreement.
- **Day 20 — который in dative + mixed.** Point 4. Test: dative relative clauses + mixed drill.
- **Day 21 — Review + diagnostic.** Prepositional vs dative contrast (в/на/о vs к/кому); audit error log; update rules card.

### Week 4 — Instrumental (Творительный, pp. 126–146) + system-wide consolidation

- **Day 22 — Instrumental nouns, sg & pl.** Урок 5, point 1: instrument/means (с/чем), быть/стать/работать + instrumental. Test: instrument & predicate constructions.
- **Day 23 — Instrumental pronouns & adjectives.** Points 2–3. Test: modifier agreement.
- **Day 24 — который in instrumental + mixed.** Point 4. Test: relative clauses + mixed.
- **Day 25 — Appendices deep-dive.** Master Приложения 1–3 (plural formation; noun declension; pronoun/adjective tables) so you can reproduce any paradigm from memory. Study Приложение 4 (p. 156, conditions of use) as the system-level summary.
- **Day 26 — Full-system mixed review.** Cross-case exercises: I give sentences requiring *all six* cases; you produce and I correct. Redo hardest error-log items.
- **Day 27 — Diagnostic essay.** Write 250+ words (analytical/technical topic) deliberately exercising every case. Self-correct, then send me for review.
- **Day 28 — Final review.** Consolidate the error log into a one-page rules card; final 20-minute test from me; decide what needs a second pass.

## 8. Testing cadence (with me)

- **Daily (optional):** I send 5–10 of my own sentences for the day's sub-topic; you write answers; I correct + give the rule.
- **Weekly:** a ~20-minute mixed diagnostic (Days 7, 14, 21) covering everything so far.
- **Final:** Days 27–28 diagnostic essay + full-system test.

I generate the test sentences myself at B2+–C1 register, keyed to the case — the book stays your self-drill source, I stay your examiner.

---

## 9. Completion criteria — you're "done" when

- You can reproduce every declension paradigm (noun/adjective/pronoun, all 6 cases, sg & pl) from memory.
- Zero case/preposition errors in the Day 27 essay and the final test.
- Your error log shows errors trending to zero week over week (the point of the log).

## 10. Interactive session format (how we actually run a day)

Each day, in chat, I run three phases in order:

1. **Drill block (~15 items).** I give 15 gap-fill phrases at B2+–C1 register for the day's sub-topic. You answer; I correct each and give the rule.
2. **Harder phase.** An extension of the day's grammar (e.g. *который* relative clauses, or a mixed sub-point). I correct.
3. **Final free-writing block.** You write your own sentences (or a short paragraph) forcing the day's case; I correct and score.

You just say the day number ("day 1") and I start. The book's self-drill (10–30 min) happens before or after, your choice.
